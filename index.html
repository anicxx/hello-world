// FROM THE FOUNDER

What worked before will not work again.

Crypto has entered a new era. The space has matured past memetic launches, retweet farming, and 'if you build it, they will retweet' GTM. Capital is more disciplined. Investors are more sophisticated. Users are harder to acquire and harder to keep. Regulation is real. The bar for what counts as a credible product, a credible round, and a credible narrative has moved, and most founders are still operating against the old bar.

AI compounds the shift. The deck is an afternoon's work in any modern tool. The memo writes itself. The positioning doc, the GTM plan, the messaging map, all of it is cheap now. The artifacts are commodity. The strategic decisions that determine whether the artifacts matter at all are not. Which investor will actually buy the narrative. What category to refuse. When the round is ready and when it isn't. What to do when the GTM motion that worked in 2019 produces nothing in 2026. These are operator decisions. They do not come out of a model.

And yet most founders drown in bias trying to make them alone. They spend every day inside the product. It's their baby. Months, sometimes years, of dedicated building. And from the inside, it becomes nearly impossible to see clearly.

Founders who closed two or three rounds get stuck on the next one. A bad investor memo will not close a warm intro. Most teams overweight marketing and underweight BD. The space has moved from let's retweet as a GTM strategy to let's close a structured deal and announce the partnership as GTM. Only seasoned operators know how to play that game.

We work with founders at the moments these patterns matter most. The raise. The launch. The pivot. The rebuild.

Not as advisors. As operators who embed, fix what's broken, and hand back a company that runs without us.

Andra Nicolau, Co-founder

// FREQUENTLY ASKED

Common questions.

What does House of Mavens do?

We are an operating team for AI and crypto founders. We embed for defined sprints, typically 6 to 16 weeks, to solve specific strategic problems: the fundraise, the launch, the positioning rebuild, the GTM motion. We fix what's broken, build the system, hire the team, and leave.

We are not a marketing agency.

Marketing agencies sell execution, content, social, PR, paid. We sell the strategic decisions and systems that determine whether the execution is worth running at all. Marketing work sits inside our engagements where useful, but it is never the engagement.

We are not a fundraising firm.

We don't broker introductions or sell our network as a service. The Round is strategic work, positioning the company so the right investors lean in, not collecting warm intros to inflate the inbox.

We are not an advisory firm.

Advisors stay. We don't. Every engagement has a defined exit, named deliverables, and a team handed off to run what we built. Recurring advisory is incompatible with the model.

We are not consultants.

Consultants produce reports. We embed, build the system, hire the team, and leave with the company running differently than when we arrived. The artifact is the operating change, not the deck.

Who do you work with?

Venture‑backed AI and crypto founders, seed through Series B. We work best with high‑conviction teams at moments where strategic clarity is the bottleneck.

What about AI? Can't I just prompt a model for this?

AI raised the floor for everyone. The artifacts, the deck, the memo, the positioning doc, are an afternoon's work in any modern tool. We get hired for the ceiling: which artifact is the right artifact, what belongs in it, which investors will actually buy the narrative, and what to do when the round doesn't move the way the deck predicted. Tools are commodity. Operating judgment is not.

Do you take equity?

Selectively. Engagement structures range from fixed‑fee project work to equity‑inclusive engagements. We discuss structure once we know the fit.

What does an engagement look like?

Every engagement begins with one diagnostic question: what is this company refusing to be? The answer reframes the work. From there, defined sprint, named deliverables, hires made if needed, full handoff at the end.

Where are you based?

New York and London. We work with founders globally.
